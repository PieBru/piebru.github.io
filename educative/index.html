Chess

Human vs AI · LLM opponents Play →

A full chess game in a single HTML file: play as Human against a hand-built browser engine (six difficulty levels), the Stockfish engine, or an LLM opponent — on either side. Optional spoken narration and AI hints + commentary make it a study partner, not just a game.

Source & docs: github.com/PieBru/AI-Chess
